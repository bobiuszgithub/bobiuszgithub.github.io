University Thesis

My university thesis focuses on reinforcement learning in a virtual meerkat simulation. I designed and implemented intelligent agents that learn social and cooperative behaviors, such as detecting predators, signaling danger to others, and coordinating food search. The project demonstrates how reinforcement learning can model teamwork and emergent behavior in artificial environments.
